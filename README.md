## ✨ Overview

**Dwarkesh Sev Usal** is a premium restaurant website built to create a strong digital presence for the brand.

The website focuses on:

- 🎬 Cinematic visual experience
- 🍽️ Food-focused presentation
- 📱 Fully responsive design
- ⚡ Fast static deployment
- ✨ Smooth animations and interactions
- 📍 Location and contact accessibility
- 📲 Mobile-friendly navigation
- 🔍 SEO-ready structure

## 🎨 Key Features

### 🏠 Cinematic Hero Section
Immersive video background with animated content and premium restaurant branding.

### 🍛 Signature Dishes
Interactive food cards showcasing selected signature dishes with visual effects.

### 📖 Brand Story
A dedicated section presenting the restaurant's identity and story through imagery and motion.

### 📋 Digital Menu
Structured menu system powered by reusable data components.

### 🎥 Cinematic Food Experience
Full-width video experience designed to highlight the food and restaurant atmosphere.

### 📍 Location & Contact
Easy access to restaurant information, location, phone numbers and social platforms.

### 📱 Responsive Design
Optimized for:

- Desktop
- Laptop
- Tablet
- Mobile

### ✨ Motion & Interactions
Built with Framer Motion for:

- Scroll animations
- Parallax effects
- Reveal animations
- 3D card interactions
- Smooth transitions
